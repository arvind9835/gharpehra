Professional deployment, not an informal sitter

Before live operations, GharPehra intends to partner with a reputed, appropriately licensed private security agency. Pehra Partners deployed to homes will be trained, certified through the partner's applicable process, identity/background verified and assigned with documented shifts.

What we expect from our security partner

  • Applicable PSARA licensing and compliance
  • Trained and certified personnel
  • Antecedent / identity verification
  • Supervisor and emergency escalation support

What the homeowner sees

  • Clear day / night / full-day assignment
  • Defined check-in and shift handover
  • Single GharPehra contact for the booking
  • Partner details confirmed before deployment
