2. Booking and acceptance

  • A booking request does not guarantee deployment until availability, location and personnel assignment are confirmed.
  • The customer must provide accurate contact, address, travel-date and access information.
  • GharPehra may decline or cancel a booking where safe/legal deployment is not possible.

3. Scope of service

The service is intended to provide visible presence, agreed access monitoring, observation, deterrence and incident escalation at the booked property. It is not a police service, emergency-response guarantee, investigation service or absolute guarantee that theft, trespass, damage or other loss will not occur.

4. Customer responsibilities

  • Secure doors, windows, valuables, vehicles and alarms before departure.
  • Disclose known hazards, aggressive pets, construction work, other occupants, domestic staff and persons who may legitimately enter the property.
  • Provide at least one emergency contact reachable during the booking.
  • Do not request unlawful duties or activities outside the approved assignment.

5. Keys and property access

The default pilot model is exterior/gate/perimeter watch without possession of house keys. If interior access is exceptionally required, the customer and service provider must complete a written key/access handover identifying the purpose, person receiving access and return process. Access codes, alarm credentials and CCTV passwords should be shared only when strictly necessary.

6. Personnel and licensed partner responsibility

The licensed partner agency is expected to remain primarily responsible for eligibility, training, antecedent verification, supervision, attendance management, uniforms/identity cards and employment/statutory compliance for deployed personnel, subject to the final signed agency agreement and applicable law.

7. Attendance, replacement and escalation

  • Personnel may be required to complete check-in/check-out and shift handover records.
  • If an assigned person is absent or unsuitable, the partner agency should provide a replacement within the service level agreed with GharPehra.
  • Serious incidents should be escalated promptly to the customer, GharPehra, the partner agency and emergency services/police where appropriate.

8. Fees, cancellations and refunds

Prices shown on the pilot website are introductory rates and may be revised before booking confirmation. Final charges, taxes, cancellation windows, refund amounts and any emergency-extension charges must be shown to the customer before payment.

9. Liability

Nothing in these terms is intended to exclude rights or liabilities that cannot lawfully be excluded. Responsibility for any incident will depend on the facts, applicable law, the conduct of the parties, the partner-agency agreement and any applicable insurance policy. GharPehra does not promise reimbursement for every loss merely because a booking was active.

10. Insurance

Where insurance or protection cover is offered, the exact insurer, policy wording, coverage limit, exclusions, deductible/excess and claims procedure will be disclosed separately. A booking should not be described as “insured against all theft” unless the relevant insurer has expressly underwritten that cover.

11. Customer valuables and loss reporting

The customer should avoid leaving unusually high-value cash, jewellery or portable valuables in an unattended property. Any suspected theft, damage or suspicious event should be reported immediately, with police reporting completed where appropriate. Claims may require evidence such as FIR/police diary, inventory, purchase proof, photographs or insurer documentation.

12. Privacy and personal data

GharPehra may process customer name, contact information, address, travel dates, booking details and security/access information only for legitimate booking, safety, support, payment, incident, legal and compliance purposes. Data may be shared with the licensed security agency and service providers only as necessary for those purposes, subject to applicable law and consent requirements.

13. Photography, CCTV and confidentiality

Deployed personnel must not photograph or disclose customer property, family, valuables, travel plans, access arrangements or CCTV/security information except where specifically authorised for attendance, incident documentation or lawful operational purposes.

14. Force majeure and unsafe conditions

Service may be interrupted or modified by events outside reasonable control, including severe weather, civil disturbance, police restrictions, local emergencies, road closures or conditions that create an immediate safety risk. Reasonable efforts should be made to notify the customer and arrange alternatives.

15. Complaints and disputes

Customers should first contact GharPehra support for service complaints and incident review. These terms do not remove statutory consumer rights or remedies available under applicable Indian law.

16. Pilot limitations

The service is currently intended only for selected locations in Bokaro. Expansion to other cities will be subject to partner availability, licensing, insurance and operational readiness.
